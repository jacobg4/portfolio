Origin Story

From Console Prototype to Blood & Burden

C++ Prototype → tiny-rpg (Python CLI) → Blood & Burden (PHP, live)

Blood & Burden didn't start as a web app. The first attempt was a rough C++ console prototype with hand-rolled combat and colored console text. tiny-rpg picked up from there in Python: a full data-driven CLI RPG with monsters, dungeons, quests, and items loaded from JSON, turn-based combat with momentum and critical hits, a spellbook, dialogue options mid-battle (taunt, intimidate, negotiate), a shop and training grounds, and save/load. Its combat pacing and quest system carried straight over into Blood & Burden's design.
